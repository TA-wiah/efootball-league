How the rankings work (tap to read)
⭐ Every team has a rating

Everyone starts at 1500. Win and your rating goes up; lose and it goes down. The points move from the loser to the winner, so beating a strong team earns much more than beating a weak one, and a bigger win counts a little more.

🔢 Example

Two teams both on 1500: the winner goes to about 1516, the loser to 1484. If a 1400 team beats a 1600 team, the winner jumps about 24 points: a surprise is worth more.

🌍 World, country, region

World is everyone on the platform. Choose a country (or a region inside it) at the top to see only teams from there: the number is the team's place in that list. The country and region come from each organization's profile.

✅ What counts

Results in public competitions. A draw on penalties counts as a draw. {% if friendly_weight == 0 %}Friendlies don't count: they have their own friendly tables.{% elif friendly_weight == 1 %}Friendlies count fully.{% else %}Friendlies count half.{% endif %} Walkovers, forfeits and abandoned matches don't count.

❓ Not on the list?

A team appears after {{ min_matches }} match{{ min_matches|pluralize:"es" }} in public competitions. Private competitions don't count, and an organization can choose to keep its teams out (Organization settings).

👟 Players

Players are ranked by goals, then assists, from the goals and assists organizers enter on match pages.

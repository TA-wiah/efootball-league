Built for eFootball

Made for eFootball: organizers set a competition up once, teams play it in the game, and everyone follows it here from a WhatsApp link.

Leagues, cups and friendliesLeagues with divisions, promotion and relegation; group stages and knockouts; friendlies inside or across organizations.
Squads from your game screenshotsUpload your Game Plan and players list: positions, ratings, starting XI and subs are filled in for you.
Fixtures in one clickFair, randomly drawn schedules, home and away, with kick-off times in everyone's own time zone.
Results you can trustTeams send the result screen from the game; the organizer confirms it; the table and top scorers update by themselves.
Rankings and awardsEvery team and player ranked across all organizations, with Player of the Year, Golden Boot and a Team of the Season.
Made for WhatsAppEvery table and match has its own link to share; reminders arrive by email and SMS.

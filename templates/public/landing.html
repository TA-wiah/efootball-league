What the platform does

A complete competition manager: organizers set it up once, and fans get a live website for every competition.

Create and manage leaguesLeagues, cups, group stages and knockouts with their own points and tie-breaker rules.
Register teams and squadsClubs, crests, home grounds and players, reused across competitions and seasons.
Fixtures in one clickFair, randomly drawn schedules, home and away, with dates and venues.
Automatic standingsEnter a score: the table, form guide and top scorers update by themselves.
Match detailsGoals, assists, cards, substitutions, referees, venues and kick-off times.
Shareable public pagesEvery table and match has its own link that looks great on WhatsApp and social media.
Staff and permissionsInvite organizers, editors and moderators, each with exactly the access they need.
Many competitions, one accountRun several leagues, seasons and organizations from the same dashboard.
